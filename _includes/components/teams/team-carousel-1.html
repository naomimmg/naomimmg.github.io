Naomi Martinez Gutierrez

I'm a quantitative methodologist in psychology. I completed my PhD in Quantitative Methods at York University in 2026 under the supervision of Dr. Robert Cribbie, and I teach undergraduate statistics at York as a Course Director.
My research asks how psychologists can draw sounder conclusions from their data. I develop and evaluate methods for equivalence testing and effect sizes (including the proportional distance, implemented in the negligible R package), study how researchers handle multiplicity, replication, and Type I errors, and apply measurement and multilevel methods to outcomes research on youth in wilderness therapy and residential treatment.
I teach statistics the way I consult: by starting from where people are. Many of my students arrive anxious about statistics and have never coded, so I build my courses around real data, R, and interactive simulations, with the goal of turning that initial fear into confidence and curiosity.
I speak English and Spanish fluently and have basic French. Outside of work, I enjoy art and sticker printing, 3D printing, journaling, and recreational ice skating.
